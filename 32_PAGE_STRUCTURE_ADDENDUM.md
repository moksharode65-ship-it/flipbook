# Master Plan Addendum: 32-Page Architecture Expansion

## Why 32 Pages?
In professional booklet and flipbook binding (both digital and offset print), page counts must follow signatures of multiples of 4 (e.g., 28, 32, 36). Expanding from 28 to **32 pages** allows full inclusion of in-depth field case studies, standard operating procedures for railway transit intercepts, and institutional CSR allocation models.
